# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Parents (mostly fathers and mothers) of a boy approaching bar mitzvah age, in Jerusalem. Their fear: the boy will stand at the Torah frozen and unprepared. Their need: that he stands on the big day calm and confident. They usually arrive from a WhatsApp link shared by another parent, on a phone.

## Product Purpose
A one-page site for Oren Mizrahi, a bar mitzvah teacher. Success is a parent understanding what Oren offers and how he differs from any other teacher, then sending him a WhatsApp message.

## Positioning
- Practice in front of a real Torah scroll, and sometimes in front of a listening audience, not only from a book.
- Kind words and encouragement throughout; mistakes are part of the process.
- Oren is 18 and has taught since 15. His age is the advantage: he is the boy's big brother.
- 23 boys have read from the Torah with him. One family trusted him twice (two brothers a year apart, shown in the photos).

## Operating Context
Lessons in Jerusalem, at Oren's place or at the student's home. Contact only by WhatsApp (050-672-3214). Parents decide quickly on mobile, often after another parent's recommendation.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static one-page site, Hebrew RTL, mobile first, deployed on Vercel at https://oren-mizrahi.vercel.app, plus /accessibility and /privacy pages.
- Approved copy is in Oren's first-person voice, parents addressed in plural.
- No price on the site. No generic slogans such as "ניסיון רב". No long dashes in text. No invented testimonials, numbers or images.
- Must meet Israeli accessibility standard IS 5568 (WCAG 2.1 AA) and keep a full accessibility statement and privacy page.
- Undecided: FAQ answers (waiting for Oren), exact subjects taught (haftarah, full parasha, blessings, tefillin, online).

## Brand Commitments
- No logo exists; the name is set as a handwritten wordmark.
- Frank Ruhl Libre must never be used (client decision).
- Signature experiences already approved by the client: opening parochet (Torah-ark curtain) the visitor drags open into light; testimonials as a WhatsApp conversation on Oren's phone; a Torah pointer (yad) that follows words on scroll; a verse that loses cantillation then vowels across the three steps.

## Evidence on Hand
- Photos (parental consent): Oren with a boy in tallit and tefillin before the ark (memorial text blurred); Oren with the boy's brother in a light suit. `public/images/`.
- Four real parent testimonials, sent for publication, shortened, pseudonymous names.
- No video, audio or further photos yet. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles
- Show, don't claim: the Torah, the scroll, the reading itself carry the proof.
- The parent's fear is answered with calm, not hype.
- Oren speaks for himself, as a big brother, warm and serious.
- Every path ends in one WhatsApp message.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
IS 5568 / WCAG 2.1 AA. Reduced motion and a site-level "stop animations" option must disable every animation. Keyboard and screen-reader paths for every interaction.
